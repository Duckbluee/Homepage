Reformulated graph neural network message passing through the lens of the Navier-Stokes equations by decomposing propagation into convection and diffusion terms.

This work introduced node-level velocity fields on graphs for adaptive, anisotropic information flow and achieved strong results across public graph benchmarks. The paper was accepted to KDD 2026.
